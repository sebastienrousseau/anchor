## Looking for a message?

Almost every page on this site describes a single ISO 20022 message, and an
address that names one requires its complete version number. `pacs.008` on its
own resolves to nothing, whereas `pacs.008.001.13` identifies a particular
message definition and therefore resolves correctly.

The [message reference](/messages/) lists all of them, organised by the business
function they perform, with a search box positioned at the top. If you know
roughly what you need but not the identifier for it, begin there.

## Looking to check a message?

Paste it into the [workspace](/workspace/), which determines what the message is,
validates it against the appropriate schema, and identifies the rule behind
every finding it reports. Nothing you paste is transmitted anywhere beyond your
own browser.
