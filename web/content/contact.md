### Report a defect

The [issue tracker](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/askiso/issues) is the public record of reported problems and requested changes. Anything filed there remains visible to everyone, including the eventual reply, which is normally preferable for a technical question.

### Report a security issue

Never in public. The [security policy](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/askiso/blob/main/SECURITY.md) explains how to report one privately, and what happens afterwards.

### Follow the releases

Every release is published with notes describing what changed and why. Watch [releases on GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/askiso/releases), or subscribe to the [feed](/rss.xml) in any reader. Selecting *Send me the release notes* in the form arranges the equivalent by email.

### Read first, perhaps

Most questions are already answered somewhere. The [frequently asked questions](/faq/) cover November 2026, privacy, schemas and cost. The [developer documentation](/docs/) covers every command and integration. Linking to the section that did not answer your question saves a round trip.

### What to expect

This is an independent open-source project rather than a vendor with a support desk. Replies come from a person, usually within a few days, and there is no ticket number. Questions that turn out to be common eventually reach the FAQ, which is the most useful destination available to them.
