## What happens now

Your message was forwarded by email, and nothing about it was retained on this
site. There is no database here and no record of what you submitted anywhere. A reply
usually arrives within a couple of working days, sometimes sooner, and always
from a person rather than from an automated system.

If you reported a defect, it helps considerably to know which version you were
running. `askiso --version` prints it, and quoting that number in a follow-up
saves an exchange establishing it.

## While you wait

- [Message reference](/messages/) — every ISO 20022 message, grouped by function
- [Documentation](/docs/) — installation, and each command explained
- [Frequently asked questions](/faq/) — the answers people need most often
- [Release notes](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/askiso/releases) — what changed, and when

If the matter concerns something plainly broken, an issue raised on
[GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/askiso/issues) is often settled
faster than correspondence, because the discussion sits beside the code
rather than apart from it.
