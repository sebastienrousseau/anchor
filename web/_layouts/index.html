Deferred · new timing by December

Unstructured addresses stop being accepted

CBPR+ — the cross-border payments and reporting rules that correspondent banks settle under — will require structured or hybrid postal addresses. Swift deferred the November cutover on 27 August and will confirm new timing by December. A message carrying a fully free-text address is still rejected by the receiving institution rather than repaired, which makes this an operations problem before it is a technical one, whatever date it lands on.
